A small recipe for the pages you need

Keep the pages. Write down the order.

A paper, a supplement, one useful appendix. Bring them into one reading packet without losing the instructions that assembled it. Page Recipe turns a short page sequence into a new PDF, locally in your browser.

No thumbnail wall, no account, no cloud upload. Just named sources, a visible sequence, and a separate output file.
